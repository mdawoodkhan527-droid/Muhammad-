# Prefit identifiability screen

## Scope and preregistration

The observation set is frozen at two closures: a qualitative mineral pattern and a post-processed δ13C carbonate mixture. Derived HI/OI, sulphate abundance, approximate sulphate δ34S, and mixing-derived values are not counted as independent targets. Conc4-A/B sulphur conflicts remain excluded from sulphur mass-balance calibration. No parameter was fitted.

The SVD distinguishability rule was declared before calculation in `identifiability_protocol.md`: whiten mineral probabilities by a unit range and δ13C by the published 2.1‰ between-sample spread, calculate sensitivities per one analyst-selected parameter screening scale, retain numerical modes when σi/σmax ≥ 10−8, and call a direction distinguishable only when σi ≥ 1. The 2.1‰ scale is not analytical precision. The parameter scales are sensitivity coordinates, not empirical probability priors.

## Structural gate

The selected model is one-way chemistry-to-mechanics. The four observations contain three mineral detection probabilities and one δ13C mixture output. None measures stress or deformation, and mechanical variables do not feed back into chemistry in this selected topology. Therefore elastic moduli, stiffness-mixing parameters, Biot coefficient, pressure/loading, and mechanical boundary conditions have exactly zero derivative with respect to the selected observations. They are structurally unobservable and were excluded before forming the chemical SVD. A direct specimen or close-analogue mechanical measurement and an independent burial-load constraint would be required to add mechanical information.

## Operators and no-fit central outputs

The mineral operator averages a logistic detection response over a boundary fraction `b/L = 0.20–0.30` and a log-uniform threshold fraction `q = 0.01–0.50` of each model phase's total inventory. The smoothing width is 0.5 in natural-log inventory fraction. These ranges are analyst-selected. The synthetic source-rich mask is not a registered bone-cavity mask.

At 40 cells, prior-averaged detection probabilities are 0.899 for barite in the synthetic bone analogue, 0.912 for barite in the matrix analogue, and 0.970 for calcite in the matrix analogue. The boundary envelope is 0.870–0.921 for bone-analogue barite, 0.890–0.931 for matrix-analogue barite, and 0.96966–0.96968 for matrix-analogue calcite. The observations describe barite as restricted to bone cavities. The synthetic model predicts a high probability of barite detection in both masks, so its current mineral pattern does not reproduce the observed localisation. This is a limitation of the synthetic domain and mask, not a fossil-specific model-data residual.

The δ13C operator is `δ13Ccalcite = f_org(δ13Corg + εSRB + εcalcite-HCO3) + (1−f_org)δ13Cbackground-calcite`. The central screen uses measured rib organic δ13C = −31.7‰, measured shale carbonate as a provisional background-carbonate proxy = −3.0‰, `f_org = 0.5` as an untuned scenario coordinate, `εSRB = 0‰`, and `εcalcite-HCO3 = +1.0‰`. It predicts −16.85‰, 3.15‰ below the published concretion mean −13.7‰. No adjustment was made to match the target. The shale carbonate proxy is not a measurement of background seawater DIC.

The `εcalcite-HCO3 = +1.0 ± 0.2‰` central factor is based on inorganic calcite precipitation experiments by Romanek et al. (1992). Heterotrophic SRB carbon isotope effects are organism- and pathway-dependent; Londry and Des Marais (2003) reported small substrate-to-CO2/biomass effects for most tested strains but also identified exceptions. Accordingly `εSRB = 0 ± 2‰` is a screening range, not a universal biological factor.

## SVD and information ceiling

There are 15 active chemical and isotope coordinates but only four operator outputs, so the local information-content ceiling is rank four. Mechanical parameters are outside this matrix because the topology gate gives exact zero sensitivity.

At 20 cells, both permeability laws give numerical rank 3 under the relative 10−8 cutoff, with singular values 3.3681, 0.06614, 3.309×10−5, and 0. At 40 cells, both laws give numerical rank 4, with singular values 3.3681, 0.06571, 1.833×10−5, and 2.044×10−6. The additional fine-grid mode is far below the predeclared σ ≥ 1 criterion. Thus the numerical cutoff rank changes with resolution, while the practical distinguishable-direction count remains one under both laws and both meshes. Only the first isotope-dominated composite direction exceeds the threshold. Its right singular vector is dominated by `f_org` (coefficient −0.979) with contributions from `εSRB` (+0.141), background δ13C (+0.141), measured organic δ13C (+0.035), and calcite-bicarbonate fractionation (+0.014). The result therefore distinguishes an effective isotope-mixture contrast, not `f_org` independently.

The second direction has σ = 0.0657 and is dominated by opposing changes in diffusivity and barite precipitation rate, with a contribution from Ba supply. The last two fine-grid directions have σ below 2×10−5 and are not distinguishable at the declared scale. The change from rank 3 to 4 at the strict numerical cutoff is resolution-sensitive; it is not evidence for an additional constrained parameter combination. The near-identical spectra under Kozeny-Carman and power-law permeability show that these observations do not discriminate between the two permeability laws in this synthetic benchmark.

The 40-cell numerical null space has dimension 11. `identifiability_nullspace.csv` lists every right-null basis vector and a measurement that would add information to its dominant parameter combination. The principal missing measurements are time-resolved, co-located porewater Ba, sulphate, sulfide/S0, DIC, alkalinity and pH; quantitative phase abundance on registered bone and matrix masks; independently constrained diffusivity and permeability; labile OM inventory and degradation; and paired δ13C of reactive substrate, DIC, background fluid, and calcite at known temperature.

## Decision

The only practically distinguishable mode is a composite carbon-isotope direction, and the calculated mineral pattern does not localise barite to the synthetic bone analogue. The current data and synthetic geometry do not identify microbial rate constants, transport parameters, Ba/sulphate supply, calcite kinetics, or mechanical properties. This is an identifiability result, not a parameter estimate or validation of the proposed taphonomic mechanism.

For mesh interpretation, the 20-cell numerical null-space dimension is 12 because the fourth singular value is exactly zero at that resolution. At 40 cells it rises to 11 under the strict relative cutoff, but the newly nonzero fourth mode remains far below the practical threshold and changes with resolution. It is therefore retained as unresolved rather than interpreted as a newly identified process. The exact mechanical zero-sensitivity result is separate from these chemical numerical null modes.
